A nest in the heart of the town

Freshly made, every day

We're a family-run café that's found its home in Needham Market's Old Town Hall — a building that's stood at the centre of town life for generations. We hope to carry a little of that spirit forward: somewhere to meet a friend, settle in with a good coffee, or treat the little ones to a bubble waffle.

Everything's made with care, the kettle's always on, and there's a warm welcome whether you're a regular or just passing through.

    Allergies & Intolerances

    If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please speak to a member of our team before you order — we're always happy to talk you through the ingredients in any dish. Our food is freshly prepared in a kitchen that handles the 14 named allergens (including gluten, milk, eggs, nuts, peanuts, soya, fish, crustaceans, molluscs, celery, mustard, sesame, lupin and sulphites), so while we take great care, we can't guarantee any item is completely free from traces.
